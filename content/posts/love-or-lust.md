I am really confused about myself, my decisions, my life, and my wishes.

Yes, it was something between us. Maybe from my side only. But still, it was there in my heart. I cannot forget the first eye-meeting, the look, her cute face, her slim, sweet, adorable body. Her pink, smooth lips. Her ponytail hair. Her body fragrance. Her cute, innocent smile. Whenever I came across her, my heart used to skip a beat. I might have died several times. I thought I could not live without her. How was I going to talk to her? How could I make her mine? Would she have accepted me? Many questions were revolving inside my mind.

Heart said to explore whatever feelings I had for her in that moment. Mind told me not to be stupid. Yes — of course heart is stupid. It was a daily fight between mind and heart. Every day I promised myself never to think about her, never to look at her. But soon I felt like breaking that promise passionately. I did not have that much strength to avoid my heart's belief in my love for her. There was not even a second when I did not think about her.

She was perfect — more perfect for me — but I was not. I hate myself when I think of being loved by her. It was a pure, foolproof dream, and I was enjoying being with her in that dream. Yes, we were more than just friends in my dreams. We were a couple. We shared our thoughts, ideas, life, happiness, sorrow together. We fought and did not talk. The very next moment we promised never to fight again. But a new fight. It just continued for infinity. If I could have had that power, I would have wished to be in my dream only, rather than being so far in reality. It gave me pleasure. Those moments were special.

I forgot what to eat, why to eat — reading, friends, home. Everything was unnecessary. I used to stay lying on her lap for hours. She used to tell me about her past, her family, her study, her future, her expectations, her wishes, her beliefs. I used to listen to her like a small kid.

"Wake up, FOOL — it's just a dream."

Someone always used to destroy my dreams.

I am not saying that she will be Miss India if she participates in a contest. But she is perfect for me — at least I think so. There are many beautiful, smart, hot girls outside. But when she is nearby, I do not feel anyone else's presence. I did not feel the same feelings for anyone that I had for her.

**TIME PASSED.**

Now I ask myself: do I really love her? Or is it just lust — attraction to her body, the attraction of a boy to a girl?

I was dying for her. Still I do, now also. But do I love her?

I used to hear from people that someone deep in love forgets to eat, forgets to drink, forgets the world, smiles always, blushes, gets lost in his or her own world. I do not feel like any of them. I do my job as usual. I am what I used to be. Maybe slight changes can be noticed, but not enough to prove that I am in love. So I am not in love with her anymore.

Really?

Then why do I think of her when someone asks about my love, girlfriend, life, future? Why does my heart still skip a beat when I see her? Why does it still believe in her? Why does it say that she will be mine?

Mine only.
