Training is over. The project hasn't started. Friends have scattered to other cities. The boy who always had a classroom to belong to now belongs to nothing except a recreation room and a phone full of cancelled plans.

Somewhere between an Xbox and a bookstore, between a bench period and a badminton court, life presses play — whether he is ready or not.
