Every heartbreak had pushed something honest into the writing. Every silence, every escape, every platform farewell — all of it became ink on a blog no one was supposed to find.

She found it anyway. And when he ran again — the same reflex, the same fear — she did not disappear. She simply waited until he remembered how to come back.
