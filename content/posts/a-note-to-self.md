I have everything I wanted, and still something feels missing.

I buy what I like. After every purchase, the same thought returns — that I need something more. Lately, things are not happening as per plan. At least, my plan. Whatever I think of, the opposite happens. I am not able to fulfil my expectations. Or if I may say it plainly, God or fortune does not seem to want me to have what I want. Things are moving very slow. I might also be doing things incorrectly.

I am stuck between ideation and prototype. The ideas I have — the todo list, you can call it — I am unable to complete. I am losing confidence in my own capabilities. I am stuck between IntelliJ IDEA, VS Code, and Eclipse. I am not getting what I want, and I do not like what I have.

But if I reflect on the words above, it is all about me. Does that make me selfish?

I used to write journals about fictional characters with a mix of reality. I had a blog, you know. It has all come down to nothing. Now I am into upgrading this, learning a new language or build tool — but it is making me frustrated. The laptop has less storage left. Email is not organised. We are using an old database. I want to explore many things, but it is not possible on the office laptop. The personal laptop is broken, and it does not have enough power to support that level of coding. I noted down, did research on good applications for writing stories, installed them — but did not use. That feel is not coming.

I am waiting for the Dell XPS 17, but it is a long wait. The XPS 15 is set to launch on 8th July on Amazon. But I like the 17-inch screen and spec. I have a plan to have my own office where I can work without getting disturbed — and once out of office, no work-related things. I will read a lot of books. I will learn Morse code. I will learn a language. I have heard learning a new language increases your IQ.

Loved ones around me are disappointed. I have lost the passion for writing, singing, reading. Now most of my time is spent on social networking, YouTube, office work, buying new things. The enthusiasm for communication is simply missing.

Should I not appreciate those who helped me in my time of need? Should I not give them my love back, show that I care, do something special for them?

Birthdays used to be a very special occasion. We were planning, preparing for it, waiting for friends and family's wishes. There used to be genuine friendship. Getting dressed for a festival, celebrating it the way we could, helping each other with cooking, cleaning, preparing — it used to be fun.

It seems the higher I go on the ladder, the less interesting life becomes. The less fascinating I am becoming. The less attractive things become — things for which I used to die. Over a period of time, that feeling changes. The thrill of going out on a ride becomes mundane. Priority shifts towards laziness.

Life is a bitch. It punches you in the face when you had just thought that now everything will be all right.

A few days before, I got to know Bhutni will join my office by the last week of September. Today I was informed that the hiring decision has been put on hold again. I mean, what the fuck. What do you want, life? Why do you show the trailer and then say the movie is never going to release?

Adding salt to the wound, her current company is giving half salary. And the timing is also odd. It creates an imbalance in work-life. Plus she is not listening to me about career change. A lot of opportunities are there outside this testing profile. And a developer is preferred — and gets more salary.
