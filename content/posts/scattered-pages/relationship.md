Relationship.

It is a word I hear often nowadays. People give speeches on it. Offer suggestions. It blows through like a western wind — through lovers, parents, adults, movies, gossip, friends' discussions. Wherever and whenever I think of it, I get confused.

At first I was not aware that the word meant anything to me. I thought I lived in my own house with parents and brothers. Unaware, I was there because they were with me.

Father for clothes, books, and walks outside. Mother for feeding me, letting me sleep by her side. Brother — a friend, actually a trustworthy friend — for games and fights, as if I were exactly where I should be. These were the people I deserved to live with. A wonderful feeling: fearing Dad, Mom's scolding, Bada Bhai's blackmail. These were not small things.

I did not know it was a family, though I did not know what family meant. I only knew they were my own — and now they are my very own.

---

It is probably difficult when you must choose between family and lover. Not love — it is typical to call your lover "love." What about your father? Your mother? Do they not love you? Do you not love them? That is what most lovers face today.

But the question before me is different: how can your lover be outside your family? How do you make a family without the loved ones already in it?

It is normal to have a close friend with whom you share important problems. Most of us do not choose our parents for that. That is why we look for someone else — often a school or college friend. It is simple for those who are open-minded and friendly. For a shy person, finding such a friend is harder than topping a math paper. They look for someone different from others who still feels like their own.

That is where we get trapped.

I do not know how friendship turns into love, how it starts, or how it ends. I am only looking for something that concerns those of us who never got the real meaning of it.

---

Helping, caring, supporting — are these signs of love? Yes, of course.

But can you not find them in the rudeness of a good heart, in a fight for your good, in a scolding for the better? It is nothing but the process of life. When you receive love, you love others — until you do not.

How big the problem, parents stand behind you. When father gets angry, mother supports. Father saves you when mother starts to scold. Brother lets you play his favorite game even though you are banned for failing an exam — though the other day he fought you for breaking his most interesting game DVD.

That is the power of relationship.

No one can break it until their own soul is destroyed by the curse of their deeds.
