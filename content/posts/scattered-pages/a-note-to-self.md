I have everything I wanted, and still something feels missing.

I buy what I like. After every purchase, the same thought returns — that I need something more. Lately, things are not happening as per plan. At least, my plan. Whatever I think of, the opposite happens. I am not able to fulfil my expectations. God or fortune does not seem to want me to have what I want. Things move slow. I might also be doing things incorrectly.

I am stuck between ideation and prototype. The ideas — the todo list, you can call it — I am unable to complete. I am losing confidence in my own capabilities. I am stuck between three code editors. I am not getting what I want, and I do not like what I have.

But if I reflect on the words above, it is all about me. Does that make me selfish?

---

I used to write journals about fictional characters with a mix of reality. I had a blog, you know. It has all come down to nothing. Now I am into upgrading this, learning a new language or build tool — but it is making me frustrated. Storage is low. Email is not organised. The personal laptop is broken. I noted down ideas, did research on good applications for writing stories, installed them — but did not use. That feel is not coming.

I have a plan to have my own space where I can work without getting disturbed — and once out of office, no work-related things. I will read a lot of books. I will learn Morse code. I will learn a language. I have heard learning a new language increases your IQ.

---

Loved ones around me are disappointed. I have lost the passion for writing, singing, reading. Now most of my time is spent on social networking, YouTube, office work, buying new things. The enthusiasm for communication is simply missing.

Should I not appreciate those who helped me in my time of need? Should I not give them my love back, show that I care, do something special for them?

Birthdays used to be a very special occasion. We were planning, preparing for it, waiting for friends and family's wishes. There used to be genuine friendship. Getting dressed for a festival, celebrating it the way we could, helping each other with cooking, cleaning, preparing — it used to be fun.

---

It seems the higher I go on the ladder, the less interesting life becomes. The less fascinating I am becoming. The less attractive things become — things for which I used to die. Over a period of time, that feeling changes. The thrill of going out on a ride becomes mundane. Priority shifts toward laziness.

Life punches you in the face when you had just thought everything would be all right.

Why does it show the trailer and then say the movie is never going to release?
