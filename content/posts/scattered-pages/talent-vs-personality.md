Today someone told me that my friends — girls — like me because of my talent, not my personality.

I am in a dilemma. Is that true, or only their view?

Then I began to analyze how both terms relate to each other, and which one best defines me. The word *talented* has dominance when associated with me. But that does not mean I lack a pleasant personality. It may not be attractive or prominent, but it is mine — completely mine.

So if someone knows me only through my talent, they must be missing me in myself.

I was always after a decent personality and a simple life with family and friends. Talent was for inner peace, or enjoyment — never the whole story.
