Another day without you.

You may not know my feelings. You may not understand my intention. I tried my best to have you in me. I dream dreams that may not come true. Your heart beats for me only — I cannot say that is true. Still my love for you flows in my blood. It is just an expression.

My parents brought me up to this level. How can I be so selfish? I never thought of them when you were in my eyes. Your love — oh, sorry, I do not know if you love me or not; maybe I am fooling myself, considering my one-sided love as yours — made me blind. I could not see them when they were suffering. I believed in you. Not in my gods. They were my gods when you were not my angel.

New things attracted me when old, portable, consistent supports were still beside me. I misunderstood them and tried to understand you — the unsolvable problem for whom I can never prepare an algorithm.

Our first meeting you can forget. I live in those memories. The only input my soul accepts after you left me at the end — not the middle — of our journey is your sweet memory. I did not dare keep you near me. Misfortune blessed me with sins; you were always there to curse misfortune and save me from my only mistake of loving you.

You are my first love.

No — second.

No — third love of my livelihood.

The other two are also in my mind. But you are primary. All others are auxiliary — backups needed when you get corrupted or power failure occurs. You knew it. Still loved me.

I love you because you love me much. Still you say I am not selfish.

I love you because I need you. Still you think I am honest.

What on this earth made you love so much that after your streaming out I cache your memories in my L2 cache? I need a hard disk to keep our fight, anger, cry, shout. I fear if it crashes. No alternatives. You are killing me. So I am too.

It was certainly my thought to take advancement in the path of sacredness.

---

I write this as someone desperate — not because desperation is beautiful, but because engineering students understand failure in systems better than failure in hearts.

An algorithm has inputs, outputs, termination conditions. Love had none of those. I fed it observations — bus window, chunni in wind, eyes meeting ten times, a tuition verandah in rain — and expected a result. The program never compiled.

I treated other girls as temporary variables. Tulika's birthday chat. Jinita's friend request. Conversations that proved I could speak when the stakes were low. With you, the stack overflowed. I crashed silently.

I neglected parents during the obsession. That is the sin no metaphor cleans. Mother reserving money from her expenditures. Father cycling fifteen kilometres. Brother sharing secrets I never returned. Younger brother playing cricket in noon heat while I sat inside dreaming of someone who did not know my name.

They were my gods when you were not my angel.

Power failed. I cached what remained. Backups did not boot. The hard disk of fights and tears I never collected — because we never fought, never shouted, never had enough real hours to fill storage.

---

After the farewell, after the pink letter, after standing like a wall while tears touched the ground, I understood something engineers learn late and lovers learn too early:

Not every process completes.

Some threads hang forever.

Some memories live in L2 because moving them to permanent storage would require admitting the program ended.

You were my primary.

The auxiliaries could not replace you.

You knew that.

Still loved me — or so Jinita's letter said, and my confession to Natasha destroyed before it could be answered.

I do not know which heartbreak was larger.

I only know both happened in the same auditorium, in the same minute, under the same clock that counted down the end of college and the beginning of everything I would write afterward.

---

It just happened.

Not because fate is poetic.

Because I loved too quietly, too long, too much in the wrong direction at the wrong time — and called it destiny when the system returned an error I should have read from the start.

The ink is here now.

The echoes came later.

Someone desperate signed this and moved on.

Not healed.

Just documented.
