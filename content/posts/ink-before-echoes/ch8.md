I had waited too long.

Now was the time for initiation — yet I could not find a way to convey what I felt to her. She was always with Jinita. Most of the time, always together. Same branch. Same tuition. Same bus if luck allowed.

So I logged into Facebook, went to the search bar, and typed: *Jinita*.

Jinita Prakash.

I paused, then sent a friend request. Now I had to wait. I browsed group pages, logged out, and let the night fill with questions: Will she accept? If yes, how will I make her understand about my sweetheart?

The next day was ordinary until tuition. I passed Neha, Suprava, and Mrinali returning from coaching without exchanging a word. Deepankar sat on the verandah, typing fast — almost certainly chatting with Ayushi Mahapatra. I sat beside him, said hi, received hi back with his angelic smile, and went inside when sir waved us in.

After class, Shreyash, Abheek, Deepankar, Tushar, and I stood near a junction discussing campus recruitment. A police van turned. A bald policeman warned us not to stand in groups.

"We are just leaving, sir," I said politely.

Tushar joked, "Let's have a fight between you two bald heads." I had shaved my head after my grandmother's death.

The policeman turned back. "What are you still doing here? What did he whisper? Do you think this is funny?"

Tushar whispered, "Let's go." We ran — Abheek and Shreyash one way, Tushar and I on bicycles toward the main road. The van followed. I told Tushar to hide in a hotel; I would take the Lochapada road and disappear into the crowd.

I reached home. The doorbell rang. The same policeman stood outside with a smile that widened when he saw me.

"So here you live?"

"Yes, sir."

"Call your father."

Before he finished, my father appeared.

"Radhakanta, what are you doing here?"

They knew each other. Dad invited him in. Ordered me to bring cold drinks. Mom served snacks. They laughed like old friends. The policeman asked my name, scolded me gently about standing in groups, showed his family photo. I looked only at the beautiful girl with blue eyes standing beside him — then compared her to Natasha and decided she was nothing compared to my darling.

---

That night, after dinner, ten notifications waited.

Five shares. Two group posts. Two tagged photos.

One friend request accepted.

I clicked only that.

Her profile was well maintained — last post two weeks old. It was her birthday. I clicked Message and copied a birthday wish from some website. Two minutes. No reply. She was not online.

Then a notification.

*Thanks.*

I expected more. I was too stupid for more. Still, I would not let the chance slip.

"Hey, birthday girl. How was the day?"

"Hi, it was good."

Cold.

"Can I have fifteen minutes… from your precious time?"

"Yah…"

I asked if the day was good because it was her birthday or if she was happy because it was her birthday. She answered the obvious way. I asked about temple, family, college friends. I used mathematical logic about OR and AND. I asked permission for a sensitive question. Then I declared time out — fifteen minutes finished — and offered extension.

She chose extend.

Why extend when I gave her escape? Did she like talking to me?

I asked favorite between papa and mom. Papa. Why papa? Because he is too good. I gave an example about my mother. She repeated my words back. I asked about books, habits, writers.

"If u don't mind why r u asking all these?"

I said I was doing a survey. Stress relief. She asked why I was stressed. I said I was still human.

I had nothing left to continue. I was not prepared to ask what mattered most.

"Ok I have to go… It was nice talking to u… Hope to talk later someday."

"Yah… I also enjoyed talking with you… bye."

---

Evening. I messaged Jinita: *Can you come online tonight? Need something to ask.*

I watched *The Vow*. After dinner I logged in. Fifteen friends online. Jinita's name glowed green.

*Friend or stranger….? Reply must.*

"Friend."

"So you have to solve my problems… as friends do."

"Ok… I will try."

"There is someone who is my inspiration… I can't tell… so how will I do it?"

Silence.

"So u r not going to help? Hun…"

"It's good, but what you want to do?"

"Just to say that I'm grateful… but I don't dare… and it doesn't let me be stable."

"Sorry, I have no idea but still I'm thinking, so give me sometime."

"Tell me the solution when u r ready."

"Yah sure."

"Well u and Natasha close friends?"

"Yah."

"Like childhood or college?"

"No no, I met her at PMEC."

"But u both r so close… I have never seen so close friendship till now."

"Thanks for compliment, but it's true."

"So u know about her very well… I suppose."

"Yah."

"So how is she… can I ask u this question if u don't mind?"

"Fine."

"No, as a person."

"Ohhh… good girl, talented, shy… sort of… but do you know her before?"

"No… but u know."

"But how do u know that I know her?"

"There is no secret… I can see u both always together… No… I'm 1000% sure because u were also coming to LK tech tuition for C++… am not I?"

"Yes, you are."

"So she is sweet, silent, responsible girl?"

"Inspiration is from friend or elders like parent?" She ignored my question. She did not like talking about anyone else.

"It depends upon who deserves to inspire others."

"Still I have no idea in my mind."

"But I think u guys r good in studies… so y can't u solve this simple problem… I always have great respect for civil branch students."

"That's nice."

"So u only can help me, will u please?"

"I want to help u but I don't know how."

"So, if I tell you the name, can u help me?"

"Maybe."

"Well u know I had 5 backs in 2nd SEM?"

"What!!!!!!!!!!"

"Then u don't know…"

"U r a LE student, na."

"WOW… it's good that even my classmates don't know that I joined the same college with them on the same day… All my fate."

"Sorry yaar."

"Why to be sorry? Everything is my fault."

"Give thanks from my side?"

"To whom?"

"My inspiration."

"U mean Natasha."

I did not answer.

"If u don't mind, how she is your inspiration, why not the BPUT TOPPER?"

Was she envious? Upset that I loved Natasha, not her?

I asked why people make idols when many famous persons exist.

"I got it. No explanation required. By the way I have some work. Bye."

Offline. Suddenly. Without waiting.

"Ok, bye."

---

Will she talk with Natasha about this?

Why did she go offline?

Why does she not like me the way Natasha might — if Natasha liked me at all?

Will she help my love story, or become the solid cause of my never-ending wait?

I did not know then what I know now.

She had a crush on me all along.

I had used the girl who loved me as a bridge to the girl who never noticed me.

That was the failed attempt — not the Facebook chat, not the indirect questions, not the sympathy about five backs.

The failure was simpler.

I asked the wrong person for the right kind of love, and called it strategy.
